Qualification / Validation Phase

This phase is intended for development, validation, and qualification before the final held-out test phase. Participants should use the public EmbodiedBench resources to build and validate vision-driven embodied agents across EB-ALFRED and EB-Navigation.

This phase runs from April 15, 2026 to May 22, 2026, 23:59 AoE. All times are Anywhere on Earth (UTC−12).

Scores from this phase are for development feedback and may be used to help organizers identify candidate teams for final held-out evaluation. Final rankings will be determined by the final held-out test phase.

Dataset, code, and baseline instructions are available at github.com/EmbodiedBench/EmbodiedBench.
