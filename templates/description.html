EmbodiedBench Challenge @ CVPR 2026

The EmbodiedBench Challenge is part of the CVPR 2026 Workshop on Foundation Models Meet Embodied Agents (FMEA) Challenges. It evaluates Multi-modal Large Language Models (MLLMs) as vision-driven embodied agents that interpret visual observations, follow language instructions, reason about the environment, and generate executable action sequences.

The challenge covers four EmbodiedBench environments: EB-ALFRED, EB-Habitat, EB-Navigation, and EB-Manipulation. EB-ALFRED and EB-Habitat focus on high-level task decomposition and planning, while EB-Navigation and EB-Manipulation require low-level action planning, spatial reasoning, perception, and precise control.

Participants may develop agents using public EmbodiedBench resources, external data, pre-trained models, and APIs, subject to disclosure in the submission metadata. Final rankings will be based on held-out test performance across the four environments.
