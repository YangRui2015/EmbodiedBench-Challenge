Terms and Conditions

By participating in the EmbodiedBench Challenge, teams agree to follow the rules of the CVPR 2026 Workshop on Foundation Models Meet Embodied Agents (FMEA) Challenges and the EvalAI platform.

  • Commercial APIs are not allowed. Participants should fine-tune their own models for embodied decision making, or design agent skills and frameworks that enable strong performance.
  • Open-source base models used by participants should be limited to fewer than 10B parameters to emphasize algorithmic design rather than model scale.
  • Teams may use external datasets, pre-trained models, and other non-commercial tools.
  • Stage 1 submissions must include the original trajectory files produced by running the official EmbodiedBench code.
  • Stage 2 submissions must be reproducible under the EmbodiedBench framework. Teams may submit either a vLLM-servable model or a custom agent framework via a modified planner/ directory. Evaluator code, environment code, and metric computation must remain unchanged.
  • Teams must not use hidden test labels, manually label held-out test examples, use human-in-the-loop labeling on the test set, or otherwise attempt to manipulate the evaluation.
  • Each team should submit under one team name. Duplicate or coordinated submissions intended to bypass submission limits may be disqualified.
  • The top 5 teams will be invited to submit their model or agent framework code (as a zip archive with README and requirements) for held-out evaluation and final ranking confirmation. Teams may also be invited to submit a challenge technical report.
  • The organizers may update the evaluation protocol, deadlines, and submission instructions before the final evaluation phase opens.

Questions can be raised through the official EmbodiedBench GitHub repository: github.com/EmbodiedBench/EmbodiedBench, through the EmbodiedBench Slack workspace at this invite link, or by contacting the organizers directly at ry21@illinois.edu.
