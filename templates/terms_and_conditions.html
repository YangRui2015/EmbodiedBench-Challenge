Terms and Conditions

By participating in the EmbodiedBench Challenge, teams agree to follow the rules of the CVPR 2026 Workshop on Foundation Models Meet Embodied Agents (FMEA) Challenges and the EvalAI platform.

  • Teams may use external datasets, pre-trained models, commercial APIs, and other tools, but must disclose major external resources in the submission metadata.
  • Teams must not use hidden test labels, manually label held-out test examples, or otherwise attempt to manipulate the evaluation.
  • Each team should submit under one team name. Duplicate or coordinated submissions intended to bypass submission limits may be disqualified.
  • Top-ranked teams may be asked to provide a short method description and reproducibility details before final ranking confirmation.
  • The organizers may update the evaluation protocol, deadlines, and submission instructions before the final evaluation phase opens.

Questions can be raised through the official EmbodiedBench GitHub repository: github.com/EmbodiedBench/EmbodiedBench or contact the organizers (ry21@illinois.edu) directly.
