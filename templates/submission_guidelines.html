Submission Guidelines

The EvalAI submission portal will be used for the EmbodiedBench Challenge at the CVPR 2026 Workshop on Foundation Models Meet Embodied Agents (FMEA) Challenges. The submission deadline is May 1, 2026, 23:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth).

For the planned prediction-file workflow, submit a single .jsonl file, or a compressed .jsonl.gz / .zip package, with one JSON object per line. Each object should include a unique sample identifier and the model prediction for that sample. 

{"sample_id":"eb_000001","prediction":"[PickupObject, PutObject, ...]"}
{"sample_id":"eb_000002","prediction":"[MoveAhead, TurnLeft, ...]"}

Each test sample should have exactly one prediction. Duplicate sample IDs may invalidate the submission, and missing sample IDs may be counted as incorrect or invalid depending on the final evaluation protocol.

Please disclose external datasets, pre-trained models, commercial APIs, simulation tools, and other major resources in the submission metadata. Do not use hidden test labels or human-in-the-loop labeling on the held-out test set.
