Evaluation Details

Submissions will be evaluated on the ability to complete embodied tasks from visual observations and language instructions. The primary reported metrics are task success rates for EB-ALFRED, EB-Habitat, EB-Navigation, and EB-Manipulation, plus an Overall score that aggregates performance across the four environments.

The leaderboard ranks submissions by Overall score. Higher scores are better for all leaderboard metrics. If needed, ties will be resolved by higher low-level task performance on EB-Navigation and EB-Manipulation, followed by earlier submission time.

We may additionally report capability-oriented breakdowns for analysis, including basic task solving, commonsense reasoning, complex instruction understanding, spatial awareness, visual perception, and long-horizon planning. These breakdowns are intended to help participants understand model behavior and may not all be used for the primary ranking.

Final held-out test set details, exact aggregation weights, and any additional evaluation protocol updates will be announced before the final evaluation phase opens. The submission deadline is May 1, 2026, 23:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth).
