Final Held-out Test Phase

This phase evaluates submissions on the hidden held-out test set for the EmbodiedBench Challenge at the CVPR 2026 Workshop on Foundation Models Meet Embodied Agents (FMEA) Challenges.

Submissions will be ranked by the Overall score aggregated across EB-ALFRED, EB-Habitat, EB-Navigation, and EB-Manipulation. Per-environment task success rates will also be reported on the leaderboard.

The final submission deadline is May 1, 2026, 23:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth). The final submission package format, result announcement schedule, and any additional reproducibility requirements will be announced before this phase opens.
